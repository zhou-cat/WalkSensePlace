Reading this map

The ceiling fixes the scale. Full saturation ≈ that many crossings overlapping within one radius. Set it once and leave it, so two neighborhoods stay comparable — otherwise the colors rescale to whatever's in view.

Radius & blur are a bandwidth choice. Widen them and everything blends toward "walkable"; narrow them and only tight clusters show. Pick values you can defend and note them.

Raw crossing density leans toward busy arterials, where marked crosswalks concentrate — it isn't a walkability score on its own.

Hover a cell for its count. This is the layer you'd feed an index — one value per fixed-geometry cell.

Only cells containing a crossing are drawn here. For the real index, generate the full tessellation in ArcGIS (Generate Tessellation → Summarize Within) so zero-count cells exist too, then join these counts.

The count ceiling fixes the color scale — keep it constant across any maps you compare.
